Avocado Toast

Home

Description

Easy, delicious, and nutritious Avocado Toast is a perfect breakfast recipe for busy mornings! Customize it with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe avocado (215g)
  • 2 slices bread toasted (I like thick-cut sourdough or whole wheat bread)
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 2 small lemon wedges
  • Flaky sea salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cut the avocado in half lengthwise and remove the pit. With a paring knife, score the avocado flesh into slices, about ½-inch thick. Using a spoon, scoop the flesh from one of the halves onto 1 of the pieces of toast. Repeat with the other half.
  2. With the tines of a fork, mash and spread the avocado slices so that they cover the toast. Drizzle both pieces with the olive oil and squeeze the lemon wedges over top. Season with salt and pepper, to taste.
